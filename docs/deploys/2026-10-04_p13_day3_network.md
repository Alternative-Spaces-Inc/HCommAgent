# 2026-10-04: HCA-P13 Day 3, the Mac app speaks to the live API with its device key

## Result

The HCA Notifier app signs its own requests and the live server accepts
the signature: at 21:50:11Z the Mac device's first call, GET
/v1/devices/self, returned 200. Its inbox and stream calls returned 403
("device is not linked to a Google account") because HCA_REQUIRE_GOOGLE_LINK
is on and the Mac device has no Google link yet; that is the Day 4 gate
working as designed. The app fell back as built: stream reconnects with
doubling backoff (observed 21:50:11, :15, :24, :41) and the 60 second poll
(21:51:15). No ask can be answered from the Mac until Day 4 links the device.

## What Day 3 contains

- HCACore: InboxModels (InboxItem, StreamEnvelope, AnswerSubmission with
  sorted keys, ActionMapper), SSEParser, InboxStore (pending set, shown
  set, deltas from a full inbox read and from stream events).
- HCANotifier: APIClient (signed GET /v1/devices/self, GET /v1/inbox,
  POST answer with 2xx/409/404-410/other outcomes, SSE stream over
  URLSession.bytes), per-ask notification categories so buttons carry the
  ask's own labels, red gates prefixed and time-sensitive, answer on tap
  with path_taken [macos_notification, banner, action] and duration_ms,
  removal of delivered notifications when an ask is answered elsewhere,
  cancelled or expired, menu lines for server state and pending count.
- gates.yml: top-level `permissions: contents: read` (closes the six
  CodeQL actions/missing-workflow-permissions alerts).
