## What is live

- Outcome deliveries are rows in webhook_deliveries, enqueued in the same transaction as the answer, cancel or expiry, and worked by the scheduled_jobs runner (kind webhook). No FastAPI background task remains.
- Each POST carries X-HCA-Delivery-Id, X-HCA-Timestamp, X-HCA-Event, X-HCA-Request-Id and X-HCA-Signature (HMAC-SHA256 under the destination secret, both secrets during a rotation overlap). Receiver contract: docs/webhooks.md.
- Destination secrets are encrypted at rest under a key derived from the api key pepper (decision 8).
- The send re-applies the SSRF rules and connects to a checked address with Host and SNI set to the registered host; redirects fail.
- Retry schedule 1 s, 5 s, 30 s, 2 min, 10 min, 30 min, 1 h (jittered); the 7th failure marks the delivery dead with a delivery.dead registry event.
